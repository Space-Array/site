November 2026

Space interferometry meeting

Mm/Sub-mm/FIR Space Interferometry: Present and Future. 15 to 19 November 2027, STFC Rutherford Appleton Laboratory, Harwell Campus, Didcot, UK.

The meeting will bring the international mm/sub-mm/FIR space-interferometry community together to discuss recent scientific results, ongoing technology developments, and future directions in the field. Topics include the imaging of the photon rings of black holes, extragalactic masers, and high angular resolution far-infrared astronomy.

10 June 2026

Space Array website launched

The initial Space Array website is now online. The site presents the long-term vision for a distributed space interferometer, introduces the SHARP mission concept, and provides information about the team and ongoing activities.
